# Truthcoin App v0.1.2

**Obliterate**, and tidier phone lists.

## New

- **Settings › Obliterate** removes what this app put on your computer, in two parts you tick, either or both:
  - **Truthcoin:** the node program the app downloaded (`truthcoin_dc`), the node's data (the chain, and your wallet with
    its seed) and the wallet's records. The panel shows what the wallet holds first (coins, shares, and anything on its
    way to eCash), and you tick that you have its recovery words: the app keeps no copy of them. Remove only this, and
    the app goes back to setting up.
  - **The app:** its settings, activity log and phone link (paired phones stop working), and what its window stored.
    As it closes, a Mac app removes its own `.app` and an AppImage its own file, where they can. Otherwise, the panel
    says to drag the app to the Trash. A `.deb` is removed with `sudo apt remove truthcoin-app`, and the panel shows
    that command.

  It lists every folder with its size before anything goes, and asks you to type `OBLITERATE`. It never touches
  BitWindow, eCash, the enforcer, or a node program you chose under Advanced.
- **Phone tab:**
  - Pairing a phone with the same name as one already listed (the same phone again, from a new Home Screen icon)
    offers to remove the old entry, showing when each was paired and last seen. Untick it if that's a different
    phone.
  - Phones are listed with the most recently seen first, and two with the same name show when each was paired.
  - Phones not seen for a week can be removed in one go.

## Fixed

- **Firefox on iPhone added the phone page to the Home Screen as a plain "T".** That Firefox seems to look for a
  site's icon at the root of its domain, not in the page: FreeBank's phone page, at its domain's root, gets its icon.
  So truthcoinapp.github.io now has the app's icon at its root. Safari reads the page's own icon and wasn't affected.

## Known limits

As in v0.1.0 (its notes): the node has no RPC login and stores its seed unencrypted; trades go through with the next
Truthcoin block; a withdrawal waits for eCash miners to approve its bundle; the Mac app isn't notarised.
