# Truthcoin App v0.1.3

**The app updates itself, and the phone says when your computer isn't answering.**

## New

- **Updates:** the app checks GitHub shortly after it starts, and twice a day while it runs. When a newer release
  carries the release key's signature, it offers **Update and restart**:
  - **Mac app and AppImage:** they download the new version and keep it only if it matches the signed checksums.
    They then put it in place of the old one and restart, with the Truthcoin node stopping and starting with them.
  - **`.deb`:** it can't replace itself, so the notice links to the release page.
  - **When the app can't update itself,** for example a Mac app run from its disk image, the notice says why and
    links to the release page.

  Settings › About can also check on demand. This is the first version that can update itself: from v0.1.2, download
  v0.1.3 by hand once.
- **The phone knows when your computer isn't answering:**
  - **The header** used to say "Connected" whenever the phone reached the relays, even with your computer off. It now
    says "Computer connected" only once your computer has answered. Otherwise it says "Computer not answering", or
    "No connection" when the phone itself is offline.
  - **A banner** says your computer isn't answering, since when, and that the Truthcoin App has to be open on it, with
    the computer awake and online. It also says how old the figures shown are, and offers **Ask again**.
  - **Home** keeps the last balance and positions, marked "as of" the time your computer last answered. "Your
    computer" says "Not answering since …" instead of the last state it reported.
  - **The status line** asks whether the app is open after 10 seconds without an answer, instead of after about a
    minute.

## Known limits

As in v0.1.0 (its notes): the node has no RPC login and stores its seed unencrypted; trades go through with the next
Truthcoin block; a withdrawal waits for eCash miners to approve its bundle; the Mac app isn't notarised.

## Verify

`SHA256SUMS` and the tag `v0.1.3` are signed with the release key in the README; each package has a GitHub build
attestation, and the Linux program and `.deb` rebuild byte for byte from the tag. See
[VERIFY.md](https://github.com/truthcoinapp/truthcoin-app/blob/v0.1.3/VERIFY.md).
